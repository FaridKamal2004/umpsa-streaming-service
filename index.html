FROM CLASSROOM TO SCREEN

A system, brought to life.

An independent portfolio enhancement of the UMPSA Streaming Service System coursework prototype. This is a working frontend demo, with no affiliation with or endorsement by UMPSA.

Built for the demo

Live search, combined genre filters, sorting, a persistent watchlist, browser-local profiles, viewing progress, and a native video player. Curated ordering adapts to genres you save or watch.

Clear boundaries

No real authentication, subscription billing, DRM, offline video downloads, or server database. Profile data stays in this browser. Film playback contacts Internet Archive media mirrors and is subject to its availability.

Film credits

Film files are streamed without modification. Artwork is original abstract CSS/SVG illustration, not official film posters. Film descriptions are original summaries. External links open the credited creators’ websites.
